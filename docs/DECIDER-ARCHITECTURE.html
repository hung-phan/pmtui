How the Autopilot Decider Works

Autopilot keeps the worker moving toward the goal. When the worker asks for a decision, a read-only decider may answer one ordinary, low-stakes question; anything that genuinely needs human authority still reaches you.

Read-only model call One stop at a time Recorded before action Validated verdict required Human owns completion

1. Contract

May

Choose one listed option or provide a bounded answer when typed policy and the goal make that action ordinary, local, and reversible.

May not

Write files, call external systems, invent options, approve hard-risk work, decide project completion, or bypass a live human attachment.

Must prove

The reply belongs to this consult, uses the same nonce and grant, stays within size/control-byte limits, and names only harness-owned options.

The decider is above Claude/Codex permission modes, not a replacement for them. Agent permissions control tool execution. The decider controls whether Autopilot can answer a worker-authored decision without escalating to the human.

2. Component topology

The important split is simple: fixed policy decides whether Autopilot is allowed to ask the decider; the decider then uses the goal and current evidence to choose. Neither layer alone can approve an action.

6. What the decider considers

Goal

What outcome the human asked the project to achieve.

Situation

The worker's current plan, recent decisions, and still-open blockers.

Question

The concrete choice, bounded options, and reported effect.

Directive

Standing human constraints that may restrict a decision but never grant extra authority.

Worker question: a validated answer is held until the ordinary idle-gated heartbeat can deliver it safely.

Terminal choice: Autopilot re-checks the live prompt, proves it can move the selection, verifies no human attached, and sends only the minimum navigation needed.

8. Non-negotiable invariants

Deterministic gate first. Model judgment cannot weaken typed policy or hard floors.
One validated verdict. Exactly one stop may flow automatically, and only after validation.
Goal-aware, not precedent-driven. The consult receives current goal, directive, bounded situation, question, and harness-owned options.
Two writers remain two writers. The decider is read-only; worker and pmd retain their file ownership boundaries.
Record before acting. The question is durable before the decider starts.
Termination precedes release. A failed kill keeps the debt and blocks the lifecycle transition.
Human attachment wins. No automated terminal selection occurs while a human owns the pane.
No machine completion. The decider cannot mark the project done.

9. Observability

The v decisions view shows the durable audit. Each row should answer:

  • Was the decider called? If not, which deterministic rule stopped it?
  • Which engine/model and target were selected?
  • What typed policy and effect metadata governed the call?
  • Was the result resolved, refused, failed, skipped, or interrupted?
  • What exact answer or refusal reason was recorded?

The audit is the source of truth and remains available after temporary decision work is cleaned up.

Durable consult debt appears as review pending. While its matching decider terminal is live, the primary session view shows reviewing, how long the consult has been open, and how many sibling decisions remain. Human-owned attention takes precedence; detailed evidence and outcomes stay in the audit.
